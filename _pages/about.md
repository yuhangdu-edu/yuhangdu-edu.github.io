# Welcome!

I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in [Management Science and Operations](https://www.london.edu/faculty-and-research/management-science-and-operations) at [London Business School](https://www.london.edu), where I am fortunate to be co-advised by Prof. [Jérémie Gallien](https://www.london.edu/faculty-and-research/faculty-profiles/g/gallien-j) and Prof. [Qi (George) Chen](https://www.london.edu/faculty-and-research/faculty-profiles/g/george-chen).

My research spans healthcare operations and global health delivery from both fairness and efficiency perspectives. I develop operational policies for maternal and perinatal care, where capacity is limited and delay is costly, with applications across health systems in both low- and high-income countries. I combine optimization, stochastic modeling, and statistical methods with primary and secondary data. My current work includes resource allocation in maternity waiting homes, the scheduling of elective Caesarean sections, and the deployment of AI-enabled portable ultrasound for antenatal care.

Field engagement is central to how I work. I collaborate closely with Liberia's Ministry of Health, Cambridge University Hospitals in the English NHS, and Amref Health Africa in Kenya. These partnerships shape my modeling and policy design. I bridge rigorous models and field implementation, combining theory and data to develop tools that are used in practice by the field partners.

Prior to joining LBS, I received my B.S. in Information Management and Information Systems from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics in 2021.
